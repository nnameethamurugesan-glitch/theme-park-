Zones & attractions

Split your day between heart‑pounding drops, gentle family rides and lazy river views. Every zone is color‑coded, clearly signed and stroller‑friendly.

  • Thrill Ridge – 6 major coasters, drop tower, neon night rides.
  • Lagoon Lights – ferris wheel, boat rides, sunset shows.
  • Tiny Tides – splash pads, mini‑coasters, soft‑play castles.
  • Haunted Harbor – story‑driven haunted house & spooky walk‑through.

Need a printed copy? Grab a free fold‑out map at the entrance ticket booths or scan the QR codes placed around the park to open this roadmap on your phone.
